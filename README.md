# Container Ship Preliminary Design Project:
## 5500+ TEU Container Carrier for China – U.S. East Coast Trade Route
## Overview  
This project presents the preliminary design and arrangement development of a 5500+ TEU container ship intended to operate on the China to East Coast of the United States trade route at a service speed of 24 knots.  

The design process was carried out using the COSCO New York (5089 TEU) as the reference/base ship. Through iterative naval architectural calculations and arrangement optimization, the vessel dimensions, hydrostatic parameters, cargo arrangement, and bulkhead placements were finalized to satisfy the operational and design requirements.  

This project was completed as a team effort of four members, with my role being the Team Lead, coordinating the design workflow, integration of calculations, and overall technical consistency of the final design.  

## Design Objectives
The primary objective of this project was to develop a container vessel capable of:
- Carrying 5500+ TEU containers
- Operating efficiently on the China – East Coast USA route
- Maintaining a service speed of 24 knots
- Ensuring adequate:
  - Stability
  - Structural safety
  - Cargo capacity
  - Compartmentation
  - Operational feasibility
- Achieving dimensions and arrangements compatible with standard container operations and port constraints

## Base Ship Selection
The following vessel was selected as the reference ship:
- Base Ship: COSCO New York
- Container Capacity: 5089 TEU

The base ship approach was adopted because it provides:
- Proven hydrodynamic performance
- Realistic dimensional ratios
- Practical compartment arrangements
- Reliable cargo distribution trends
- Established operational characteristics

Using an existing vessel as the starting point significantly reduced uncertainty during the preliminary design stage.

## Design approach
### The design process involved:
- Selecting a suitable base ship
- Estimating principal dimensions
- Iteratively refining:
  - Length, breadth, depth, and draft
  - Displacement and hydrostatics
  - Cargo capacity
  - Bulkhead placements
  - Internal compartment arrangements
  - Powering calculations
  - Aft and transom design
  - Bulbous bow dimension calculations
- Verifying stability and operational feasibility
The final design successfully achieved the target capacity of 5500+ TEU while maintaining the required speed and overall design consistency.

### Bulkhead & Cargo Arrangement
Special attention was given to:
- Collision and watertight bulkhead placement
- Cargo hold optimization
- Container stacking arrangement
- Structural compartmentation
- Longitudinal balance and stability

The arrangement was refined iteratively to improve both cargo efficiency and safety.
